## 2. Core premise and themes

**Premise.** In the Ashen Archipelago, only the nine noble houses may hold a *Current*, the living power of water.
Rinka Shiomi is a 17-year-old fisher girl with no name, no money and no permission. When the sea drowns her village
and the fortress takes her little brother Tomo as "tide-sick", her own wild Current breaks loose. To get him back she
enters the Ascension Trials: a climb through nine fortresses that no commoner has ever survived past the first.

**The five themes**
1. **The gate is rigged.** Society is built to keep people like her out; every fight is preceded by visible
   gatekeeping.
2. **No heritage, no permission, no apology.** Rinka isn't proving she deserves to exist; she's proving nobody had
   the right to decide that.
3. **Stolen water.** The nobles' power was taken from the common people; each fortress peels back the lie.
4. **Kindness can be a weapon.** The person who believed in her most was using her.
5. **Found family.** Misfits who were thrown away choose each other.

## 3. World rules and power system

**The Currents (潮流, *chōryū*)**
- The living force of water: tides, rain, ice, mist, blood and breath. A holder commands one *expression* of water.
- **Law of Lineage:** only noble blood may hold a Current. A commoner who shows one is **tide-sick (潮病み,
  *shioyami*)** and is taken to the fortresses, never to return.
- **Cost:** using a Current drains the body. Nobles are trained to ration it; Rinka isn't, so big releases cost her
  nosebleeds, shaking hands and blackouts.

**The Fortresses (城塞, *jōsai*) and the Ascension Trials**
- Nine fortresses on nine great islands, each ruled by a **Warden**, the strongest heir of its house.
- Once a generation any candidate with a noble **sponsor** may challenge the Wardens in order. Beating a Warden
  earns passage to the next fortress; beating all nine earns a seat on the Tidal Council.
- **The secret (revealed slowly):** each fortress stands on a **seal** holding back the sea's Current; the nobles
  draw the sealed power into themselves, and the taken "tide-sick" are drained to keep the seals fed. Rinka's Current
  is the seal cracking, and the surges drowning villages like hers are the sea pushing back.

**Visual energy identifiers**

| Current | Who | Look | Colour |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Wild Tide** | Rinka | Crashing waves, boiling foam, bursts up through floors and stone; never clean | Deep sea-blue + white foam |
| **The Crest** | Seiran (1st Fortress) | Silent glassy mirror panes, razor-thin laminar streams | Clear, pale cyan edges |
| **The Rime** | Lady Kagari Mibu (2nd Fortress) | Water flash-frozen into lilac ice spikes and creeping frost | White + lilac |
| **The Squall** | Lord Raizen Kuze (3rd Fortress) | Driving sideways rain, scalding steam, a pressure-drop roar | Storm violet-grey |
| **The Mist** | Lady Hisui (sponsor) | Fine silver fog that hides and reveals | Silver; sickly green at the betrayal |

**Core rule for every fight:** noble Currents are controlled and beautiful; Rinka's is raw and ugly-strong. The
visual contrast *is* the theme.

## 4. Character roster (phase 1)

**Rinka Shiomi (汐見 凛花), 17, protagonist.** Fisher girl from Kurose. Stubborn, blunt, accidentally funny,
furious at cruelty; hides fear under bravado and cries only alone. Low, wide "rocking deck" stance; bare-handed;
short wind-up, then everything at once. One signature strike, not combos. Voice: the locked hero voice (bitter calm
/ raw shout).

**The misfit crew**
- **Tomo Shiomi (汐見 友), 10, the stake.** Her brother. Gentle, draws sea creatures on everything, wears the wooden
  fish Rinka carved. Taken in episode 1.
- **Yuzu Hayami (早見 柚子), 16, the brains.** A commoner lantern-keeper inside the First Fortress; sarcastic,
  secretly brave; knows the hidden passages and keeps a notebook of everything the nobles hide. Carries the mystery's
  clues.
- **Gonta Iwabe (岩部 権太), 34, the shield.** Huge dockhand who lost his own daughter to the fortress. Loud, kind,
  always eating, cries at everything; fights gently with a mooring chain.

**Lady Hisui Amagiri (天霧 翡翠), 26, the sponsor, mentor and betrayer.** Heir of the weakest noble house. Charming,
witty, warm, and treats commoners like people. Sponsors Rinka when no one else will. Her closed fan hides a sealing
tool. **Secret:** she needs a wild Current strong enough to crack seals so her house can seize a reservoir; every
victory Rinka earns opens a door Hisui wanted opened.

**The Wardens of Season 1**
- **Seiran Takatsuki (高槻 青嵐), 21, 1st Fortress, the Crest.** Cold, precise, bored by commoners, secretly
  crushed by his family's expectations. Reluctant ally in Season 2.
- **Lady Kagari Mibu (壬生 篝), 30, 2nd Fortress, the Rime.** Patient, sadistic duelist who freezes opponents slowly
  and calls it mercy. Her fortress keeps the drained "tide-sick" frozen.
- **Lord Raizen Kuze (久世 雷閃), 45, 3rd Fortress, the Squall.** Veteran war-noble, honourable and terrifying; the
  first noble to treat Rinka as a real opponent. Dies holding the seal in the finale.

## 6. Audio and visual style guide

**Opening (OP), ~1:30, reused every episode.** J-rock, 160–180 BPM, driving drums, distorted guitar, soaring hook.
- 0:00–0:10 a single wave-crash hit on black; the logo shatters in.
- 0:10–0:40 verse: Rinka running along the shore, the crew, fortress silhouettes.
- 0:40–1:05 pre-chorus: the Wardens in sequence, each in their Current's colour.
- 1:05–1:25 chorus: Rinka's strike, waves rising behind her.
- 1:25–1:30 final impact frame → title.
- Hidden hint: Hisui appears smiling, but her shadow points the wrong way.

**Ending (ED), ~1:30, with credits.** Orchestral rock / ballad, 90–110 BPM, melancholy but hopeful. Slow pans across
Tomo's sea-creature drawings coming to life; Rinka alone on the drowned pier at sunset; the crew joins one by one in
the final shot. Credits roll on the right.

**Character leitmotifs and OST direction**

| Character | Motif |
|---|---|
| Rinka | Taiko + distorted guitar riff; builds from a single drum heartbeat to full band at her strikes |
| Tomo | Music-box fisher's lullaby; returns in the finale on full orchestra |
| Seiran | Celesta and glass harmonica with sterile high strings: beautiful and cold |
| Hisui | Warm koto and soft strings; at the betrayal the same melody turns minor on detuned strings |
| Kagari | Sparse plucked notes over an icy, empty drone |
| Raizen | Low brass and war drums, noble and heavy |
| The crew | Light shamisen and woodwind comedy theme |

**Rule of silence:** the music cuts out one beat before every big impact; the hit lands with the sound.
Music is made with local AI / MIDI tools and judged by ear against CC0 references before it is locked.

**Colour palettes**
- **The world:** ash grey, slate sea, lantern amber. Warm light = people; cold light = nobles.
- **Rinka:** navy, rope-tan, sea-blue foam.
- **The nobles:** clean whites and jewel tones (indigo, lilac, violet); fortress interiors in lacquered dark wood lit
  by lanterns.
- **The betrayal:** Hisui's jade turns sickly green and her silver mist turns grey; the palette tells you before the
  dialogue does.

**Action and framing rules**
- Fights: **tension → one devastating exchange → aftermath.** Standoffs, eye close-ups and huge single impacts: an
  inked impact frame, a flash, stepped shake and the hit sound on the same frame.
- Emotional scenes stay in close-up, with the speaker's mouth on screen whenever they speak.
- One character per drawing for dialogue; contact shots are silent wide shots or silhouettes.
- Every episode: a hook in the first 30 seconds, a turn every 3–4 minutes, one hype moment and a cliffhanger.

**Dual-market production principles**
- **Global:** a clear injustice, a relatable underdog, fast hooks and cliffhangers for binge-watching.
- **Japan:** seasonal and coastal motifs (lanterns, festivals, fishing life), honorifics in the Japanese dub, an
  eyecatch at the half, and a next-episode preview. A Japanese dub with English subtitles is possible later with the
  pipeline's Japanese voices.

## Production rules (locked)
- Pure 2D anime with a Ufotable-style digital finish; never 3D-looking. Shots render with `scripts/cel_shot.py`.
- Characters move only as rigid cut-outs; a drawing is never bent. Hair motion comes from extra drawings.
- Original characters only: no demons, no swords for the leads, no breathing techniques, nothing from existing
  franchises.
- Rinka is voiced by the locked `qwen3` voice `hero` (assets/voices/hero_ref.json).

## Audience rationale (owner's decision criteria)
- **Who:** 14-24, worldwide, phones and laptops; underdog battle fans + webtoon betrayal-drama readers. Girls are
  ~44% of teen anime fans, yet girl-led battle shonen is rare.
- **Demand vs supply:** action/battle leads in every market; YouTube-original animation is watched weekly by most
  14-24 animation fans; serialized cinematic original anime on YouTube is thin.
- **Problem it speaks to:** *the system is rigged by birth, and I was told I'm not enough.*
- **Engagement engine:** nine fortresses = nine countable goals + a three-season mystery + a personal betrayal.
