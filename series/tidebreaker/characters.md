# TIDEBREAKER — Cast

Designs are drafts until the owner's 2D sketches are approved; then `characters.json` gets the canonical tags and
locked reference images. Colours are listed so every drawing (and every sketch tool) matches.

## Rinka Shiomi — the hero
- **Age / role:** 17, fisher girl from Kurose, a village on the shore of the First Island. No lineage, no sponsor,
  no permission. Voice: the locked `hero` voice (bitter and quiet when hurt, a raw shout when she breaks loose).
- **Want / need:** wants her brother Tomo back and a seat no commoner has held; needs to learn she isn't proving
  she deserves to exist, she's proving nobody ever had the right to decide that.
- **Personality:** stubborn, blunt, funny without meaning to be, quick to anger at cruelty, terrible at being
  polite to nobles. Hides fear under bravado; cries only when alone.
- **Look:** athletic but small; sun-browned skin; messy, wind-whipped **black hair to the shoulders** with a
  short side braid tied with fishing twine; **sea-grey eyes**, fierce and unblinking; a thin scar across the left
  knuckles. Practical fisher clothes: sleeveless dark-navy tunic, faded rope belt, cropped grey trousers wrapped
  at the shins, bare forearms with rope-burn marks, worn straw sandals. Colour: navy, grey, rope-tan.
- **Stance / fighting:** low and wide, like standing on a rocking deck. Bare-handed, no forms; short wind-up,
  everything released at once. Her signature is one huge strike, not a flurry.
- **Current: the Wild Tide.** Volatile, violent water: it crashes, foams, boils and bursts up through floors and
  stone. White foam and deep sea-blue, never clean. She can't fully control it yet; it costs her (nosebleeds,
  shaking hands).

## Tomo Shiomi — her little brother (the stake)
- **Age / role:** 10. Showed a Current first; taken to the First Fortress as "tide-sick" in episode 1.
- **Personality:** gentle, curious, draws sea creatures on everything. The one person Rinka is soft with.
- **Look:** small, thin, the same black hair cut short and uneven, big grey eyes, oversized hand-me-down tunic,
  a carved wooden fish on a cord around his neck (Rinka made it). Colour: faded blue, wood brown.

## Seiran Takatsuki — Warden of the First Fortress (first rival)
- **Age / role:** 21, heir of House Takatsuki, the youngest Warden in a century. Season 1's first wall; later a
  reluctant ally once he learns what his house's Current really is.
- **Personality:** cold, precise, bored by commoners, privately exhausted by the expectations he was born into.
  Thinks fighting is a chore beneath him, until Rinka makes him bleed.
- **Look:** tall, slender; pale skin; straight **silver-white hair** cut neatly at the jaw; narrow **indigo eyes**;
  an indifferent half-lidded gaze. Pristine structured ceremonial armour in white lacquer over deep **indigo**
  robes, a silver tide crest on the chest, long sleeves. Hands often behind his back. No weapon.
- **Fighting:** minimal, geometric: he barely moves. Deflects with tiny hand movements.
- **Current: the Crest.** Glassy, silent, perfectly clear laminar water: floating mirror panes and razor-thin
  pressurized streams, exactly where he wills them. Colour: clear with pale cyan edges, never foam.

## Lady Hisui Amagiri — the sponsor (the betrayer)
- **Age / role:** 26, heir of House Amagiri, the weakest and poorest of the nine noble houses. Sponsors Rinka into
  the Trials when no one else will; becomes her mentor and the warm heart of Season 1; betrays her in the finale.
- **Personality:** charming, witty, generous, treats commoners like people, and means it, which makes the betrayal
  hurt. Patient. Always three moves ahead.
- **Look:** elegant; warm medium skin; long **jade-green hair** in a loose low braid over one shoulder with a gold
  hairpin; soft **amber eyes**; a kind smile. Layered robes in jade and cream with gold trim, a fan she never
  opens (it hides a sealing tool). Colour: jade, cream, gold.
- **Current: the Mist.** Fine silver fog that reveals and conceals; she rarely fights.

## Yuzu Hayami — the lantern-keeper (crew)
- **Age / role:** 16, a commoner servant who tends the First Fortress's lanterns. Knows its hidden passages and
  where the "tide-sick" are kept. Joins Rinka; the brains of the crew and the keeper of the mystery's clues.
- **Personality:** sharp-tongued, sarcastic, secretly brave; keeps a notebook of everything the nobles hide.
- **Look:** small and wiry; short **orange-brown bob** with a soot smudge; **green eyes**; round glasses; grey
  servant's kimono with sleeves tied back, a lantern pole on her back, soot-stained gloves. Colour: grey, orange.
- **No Current** (as far as she knows).

## Gonta Iwabe — the dockhand (crew)
- **Age / role:** 34, a huge dockworker from Kurose who lost his own daughter to the fortress. Rinka's
  self-appointed bodyguard; comic relief with a broken heart underneath.
- **Personality:** loud, kind, eats constantly, cries at everything, would walk into the sea for Rinka.
- **Look:** very tall and broad; tanned; shaved head with a short dark beard; small kind eyes; sleeveless work vest,
  a towel around his neck, rope-wrapped forearms, a faded anchor tattoo. Colour: brown, off-white.
- **No Current.** Fights with a mooring chain, carefully, because he hates hurting anyone.

## Lady Kagari Mibu (壬生 篝) — Warden of the Second Fortress
- **Age / role:** 30, heir of House Mibu. Season 1's second wall (episodes 5–8). Her fortress keeps the drained
  "tide-sick" frozen in the Frozen Ward. Gives Rinka her first real defeat (ep 7), loses the rematch (ep 8).
- **Personality:** patient, elegant, quietly sadistic. Freezes opponents slowly and calls it mercy. Never raises her
  voice; enjoys watching people realise they've lost.
- **Look:** tall and slim; very pale skin; long straight **lilac-white hair** in a severe high bun with two loose
  strands framing the face; **pale violet eyes**; a calm, cruel half-smile. Fitted white and lilac layered noble
  robes with a high collar, silver snowflake-crest clasps, long sleeves edged in frost blue. No weapon.
  Colour: white, lilac, frost blue.
- **Fighting:** a poised duelist; one raised hand, frost forming at the fingertips. She doesn't chase; she makes the
  floor, the air and her opponent's limbs freeze around her.
- **Current: the Rime.** Water flash-frozen into lilac ice spikes, creeping frost and frozen walls. Colour: white
  and lilac, crisp and glittering. Leitmotif: sparse plucked notes over an icy, empty drone.

## Lord Raizen Kuze (久世 雷閃) — Warden of the Third Fortress
- **Age / role:** 45, head of House Kuze, a veteran war-noble. Season 1's final Warden (episodes 9–12): the first
  noble to treat Rinka as a real opponent; yields in their trial when he sees what her Current really is, and dies
  holding the seal shut during Hisui's betrayal in the finale.
- **Personality:** honourable, blunt, terrifying in battle and gentle out of it; respects strength wherever it is
  born. Carries guilt about what his house has done and has never said it aloud.
- **Look:** very tall and heavily built; weathered tan skin; swept-back **dark grey hair streaked with silver** tied
  in a short tail; a short grey beard; a long old scar across the right eyebrow; **steel-grey eyes**. Dark
  violet-grey lamellar war armour over a storm-grey robe, a rain-cloud crest on the shoulder guard, a tattered dark
  cloak. No weapon. Colour: storm violet-grey, steel, dark cloak.
- **Fighting:** stands like a mountain, arms folded until he decides to move; when he does, the storm moves with him.
- **Current: the Squall.** Driving sideways rain, scalding steam and a pressure-drop roar that shakes the hall.
  Colour: storm violet-grey. Leitmotif: low brass and war drums, noble and heavy.

## The Narrator
An older Rinka telling the story looking back (voiced by the locked hero voice in its calm delivery, so the
narrator video and the series share one voice and one secret: she survives). Decided for the narrator video; can be
changed before it's recorded.
